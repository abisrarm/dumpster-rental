Questions

FAQ

How do I get a price?

Every rental price is on its card above, and it covers the drop, 7 days, and the haul. For junk removal, or if you're not sure what you need, send photos or a video above or call and we'll give you an exact number.

What size dumpster do I need?

Most garage and closet cleanouts fit a 10 yard. Kitchen remodels and basements usually need a 15. Whole-home cleanouts and flooring jobs fit a 20. Big renovations call for a 30, and a 40 handles bulky, light loads. Not sure? Send us a photo.

Do you do junk removal without a dumpster?

Yes. If you'd rather not touch it, our crew comes out, loads it, and hauls it away the same visit.

How long can I keep the dumpster?

Every rental includes 7 days. If the job runs long, call and we'll extend it, or swap a full can for an empty one.

Will the dumpster damage my driveway?

We set it down gently and put it where you tell us. If you're worried about your driveway, say so and we'll talk through the safest spot and protecting the surface under the rails.

Do I need a permit?

Usually not if it sits on your own driveway or property. Putting it on a public street can need a permit in some cities. Ask when you call and we'll tell you what applies where you are.

How full can I fill it?

Keep everything level with the top rail. Overfilled cans can't be hauled safely, and we may have to take the extra off before we load it.

What can't go in?

No paint, chemicals, fuel, tires, batteries, propane tanks, asbestos, or anything with refrigerant. If you're not sure about something, call before you toss it.

What areas do you serve?

Austin metro and all of Hays County
